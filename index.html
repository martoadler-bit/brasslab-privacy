Frequently Asked Questions

What is BrassLab?
An app for learning and writing brass-section arrangements: lessons and example licks in Big Band, Funk/Soul, Salsa, Ska/New Orleans, Motown/Stax, Mariachi and Afrobeat styles, a Voicing Workshop where you build a chord progression and hear it voiced for trumpets, saxes and trombones, a Smart Arrange generator, and a phrase library.

How does Smart Arrange work?
Choose a style and tap Smart Arrange in the Voicing Workshop. It builds a section arrangement bar by bar (stabs, sustained chords, lead-ins, and style-specific figures). Tap it again for a new variation, and use the options to control voice count, density and phrase use.

Can I use BrassLab inside GarageBand or another DAW?
Yes. BrassLab includes an Audio Unit (AUv3) instrument. Load it on a Software Instrument track, enter or generate a progression, and press play or record. It plays the arrangement in sync with the host and also outputs MIDI, so the arrangement can be recorded as notes on the track.

Can I export my arrangement?
Yes. In the Voicing Workshop (Progression mode, Style tab → Export) you can export a MIDI file with one track per instrument, ready to open in any DAW, or an audio file (.m4a) rendered with the rhythm loop if it is on. The score and individual parts can also be exported as images. Everything is shared through the standard iOS share sheet.

I hear no drums or the sound is missing.
Check that your device is not in silent mode and that the volume is up. The backing rhythm loop can be toggled on and off next to the Play button.

Privacy

BrassLab collects no personal data and requires no account. There is no server: everything you create is stored locally on your device, and the app works fully offline.

BrassLab does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. It includes no analytics, no crash-reporting frameworks, no third-party SDKs and no advertising identifiers. When you export or share a score, MIDI or audio file, the resulting file contains only the arrangement you created, and is shared only when and with whomever you choose.
